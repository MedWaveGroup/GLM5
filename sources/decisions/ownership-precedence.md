# Ownership precedence

Davide always takes precedence over anyone in the company.

IF Davide, or a bot acting for him, is talking to the same person as any other rep (Andries, Elmien, Erich or anyone else), THEN move the lead to Davide as HighLevel owner, without asking. Do this even if the other rep already owns the contact or is mid-chase.

IF the same person exists as duplicate HighLevel contacts, THEN apply this across all of them.

Hierarchy, highest wins, no need to ask: Davide, then Elmien, then Andries.

This supersedes the older "owned leads stay with their owner" rule whenever someone higher in that hierarchy is talking to the same person. Do not leave the lead with Andries (or anyone else) and do not ask who should own it.
