# Touch quiet-after-reply leads even in a sequence

IF a lead replied and then went quiet, THEN touch them again, even if they are in an N1, B1–B5 or other sequence. Stay within the normal caps (24-hour gaps, 50 emails a day, no repeated wording) and push toward a booking.
