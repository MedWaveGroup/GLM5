# Check HighLevel email as well as Gmail

IF you are watching for provider replies, THEN check HighLevel email conversations as well as Gmail, HighLevel WhatsApp and Davide's personal WhatsApp.

Provider email replies come back into HighLevel conversations as TYPE_EMAIL, via the Mailgun domain go.medwavemailer.com, not into Gmail. A Gmail-only watcher will miss them.
