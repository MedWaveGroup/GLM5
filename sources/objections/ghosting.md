# Ghosting

Use when a lead has gone silent after two or more attempts. Stay helpful, never guilt-trip. No draft until the lead has a value score, unless you are using Davide's first verbatim line.

## Trigger phrases

- no reply after brochure, webinar or call offer
- ghosted, no words
- tried calling and texting, haven't heard back

## Rule

- Helpful, never guilt-tripping.
- One question per message. No score means no new draft until Context bot scores the lead.
- No reply after this → Give Give Give Take / Outreach nurture. A reply goes back to the setter.

## Approved reply wording

- Davide verbatim first reply: "Hi {first name}, I've tried calling, I've tried texting, haven't heard back from you, where do we go from here? Happy to help either way 🙂"
- Follow-up: "No pressure at all. Here's something useful in the meantime: {approved brochure}. Our aim is simple: an easier life for you. Shall I check back in a few weeks, or would you rather I close your file?"
- Low score 1-3: "Hi {first name}, no need to reply to this one. I thought you'd find our brochure useful for your patients: {approved brochure}. If any questions come up, I'm here 🙂"
- Mid score 4-6: "Hi {first name}, I've tried calling, I've tried texting, haven't heard back from you. Would a quick call with Andries or Elmien help, so you can see how it works?"
- High score 7-10: "Hi {first name}, I've tried calling, I've tried texting, haven't heard back from you. Would a quick call with Andries or Elmien help? They can walk you through how other practices set up and run sessions day to day."
- SA high band, optional proof, SA only: "Hi {first name}, a quick one: 750,000+ patients. 500+ clinics. If you'd like to see what's behind that, Andries or Elmien can take you through the options on a quick call. Shall I set one up?"

## Do not say

- Peer intros or "system near you" (HOLD)
- The 750,000+ patients line in US copy
- Guilt, pressure, or a second question in the same message
