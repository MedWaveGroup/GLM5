# Low patient volume

If a practice says they do not see enough patients, ask for a weekly number first. Low volume goes to nurture. Growth intent goes to a closer. Do not claim that the system includes marketing.

## Trigger phrases

- we don't see enough patients
- my patient volume is too low
- I don't have the numbers for this
- we're a small practice

## Rule

- Ask how many patients they see a week. Do not use try-before-you-buy (HOLD).
- Do not say "the system includes marketing" or "we help practices bring new patients in" (HOLD).
- Low volume → Give Give Give Take nurture. Growth intent → closer call.

## Approved reply wording

- First reply: "Thanks for being straight with me. Roughly how many patients do you see a week at the moment?"
- Follow-up: "Thanks, that helps. Would a quick call with Andries or Elmien help? They can walk you through how other practices set up and run sessions day to day."

## Do not say

- Marketing-inclusion or patient-acquisition claims (HOLD)
- Peer intros (HOLD)
- Try-before-you-buy, prices, earnings or payback
