# Budget / too expensive / cheaper alternative

For B2B leads, a budget objection is answered with value (system, not a box) and a call, never with a figure. For confirmed patients, use the separate [[patient-price]] line, and if they still say it is too expensive, send the providers link and close contact.

## Trigger phrases

- it's very expensive for now
- way over my budget
- can't afford it at the moment
- extremely expensive
- can't take the financial risk at this stage
- I heard it costs R… (price hearsay)
- cheaper alternative / I can get a cheaper device

## Rule

- B2B: no prices, deposits, rental amounts, earnings or payment terms in the reply. Offer a closer call.
- Never confirm or deny a overheard price.
- Confirmed patients: use [[patient-price]], not these B2B lines.
- Score low: build value first with the approved brochure. Score 6 or more: isolate and hand to a closer.

## Approved reply wording

B2B first reply: "Totally understand, it's a real investment. We don't sell a box, though. Every package includes delivery, installation and training for your team. Is it the overall amount, or more about how it's paid?"

- Cheap alternative follow-up: "Would a quick call with Andries or Elmien help, so you can see exactly what's included?"
- Can't afford it at the moment: "Thanks for being honest, I appreciate it. Is it that you're not sure it would fit your practice, or is it purely the timing of the cash?"
- Interested but extremely expensive: "Totally hear you. Before we talk numbers, can I ask what you'd want it to do for your practice? Then Andries or Elmien can show you the option that fits."
- Can't take the risk: "That's sensible. Roughly how many patients do you see a week? Then Andries or Elmien can take you through the options that fit."
- Price hearsay: "Happy to clear that up properly. Andries or Elmien can walk you through the actual options and send a written quote. Shall I set up a quick call?"

## Do not say

- Any full equipment list price (R100,000, R190,000 and similar are SUPERSEDED 12 Nov 2026 10:21 SAST). See [[monthly-prices-only]].
- Monthly or finance amounts to a CPD-list doctor who has not attended a MedWave online event (see [[cpd-no-pricing]])
- Any price, deposit, rental amount or earnings figure to a B2B lead who should be booked on a call instead of quoted in writing
- The patient price line to a practitioner, or to a CPD-list doctor under the no-pricing hold
- Full package marketing claims that are still on HOLD
- Try-before-you-buy (on HOLD)
