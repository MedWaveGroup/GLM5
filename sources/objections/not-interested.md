# Not interested

A "not interested" reply is a possible opt-out. Check the suppression list and HighLevel DND first. STOP or unsubscribe gets no reply.

## Trigger phrases

- Not interested
- No thank you
- I have decided not to invest
- following internal discussions, decided not to proceed
- STOP / unsubscribe / Reply 1

## Rule

- On any opt-out list: no message. Mark excluded and keep DND.
- Not on a list: the one-time confirmation below is the only allowed reply, and only if it has not already been sent. Then DND, after Davide approves the handling.
- "Just reading up" or "email me info, no call" is not the same as not interested.

## Approved reply wording

- Only allowed confirmation: "Understood, thanks for letting me know. You won't hear from MedWave Group again. – [Name], MedWave Group."
- Just reading up / email me info, no call: "Of course, no call needed. I'll send the brochure here. Is there anything specific you'd like it to cover?"
- STOP / unsubscribe / "1": no reply. Opt-out handling only.

## Do not say

- Any follow-up, brochure or isolate question after a clear "not interested"
- Davide's take-the-blame line (failed QA, internal HOLD only)
- A Reply 1 footer on the confirmation message; that one line is the exception to the usual sign-off
