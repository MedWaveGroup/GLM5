# Finance options / no-money-down (healthcare providers)

A "Finance options" or "More info on Financing" tap is buying interest, not an objection. Route it to Davide or a closer the same weekday. The no-money-down soft line is only for confirmed healthcare providers.

## Trigger phrases

- Finance options / More info on Financing (button tap)
- do you have finance or rental options
- no money down
- how do I pay for it (from a practitioner)

## Rule

- Healthcare providers only for the no-money-down line. Never use it with patients.
- Never mention 90 days, an interest rate, a term length (including 60 months), or any payment terms in lead-facing wording.
- Do not put a rental amount in lead copy unless Davide has approved the figure and Ian's disclaimer goes in word for word. The generic rental-term holding line is still on HOLD.
- No finance, credit, instalments, payment plan or "own it for" in lead copy. Internally it can still be called finance; in copy it is rental only.

## Approved reply wording

- No-money-down line, healthcare providers only, word for word: "We have no-money-down options for practitioners like yourself. Would you like to hear about our flexible options?"
- Generic rental holding line is on HOLD until Ian fills the term, and must not be sent as written: "Thanks {first name}, great question. There's a [RENTAL TERM – pending Ian] option, and [CLOSER] will send you a written quote with the full terms. What's the best time today for a quick call?"

## Do not say

- 90 days, interest, 11%, 60 months, credit, instalments, payment plan, or "own it for"
- Full equipment list prices (SUPERSEDED 12 Nov 2026 10:21 SAST). If a non-CPD lead may be quoted, use only the monthly amounts in [[monthly-prices-only]]
- Any monthly or finance amount to a CPD-list doctor who has not attended a MedWave online event
- Any rental or deposit figure without Davide's approved number plus Ian's disclaimer
- The no-money-down line to a patient
