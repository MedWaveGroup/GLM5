## Local summary (qwen2.5:3b, unverified — check against facts below)

- **Opt-Out Contacts**: Contacts opting out are kept in HighLevel with DND on and tagged as "Opted out." They are never deleted or messaged.

- **Meta Payments**: Only accounts/emails naming MedWave count for Meta payments; ignore the old account.
- **Marketing Content**: Marketing content must be B2B only, not consumer-facing, and avoid showing the device beating people-in-treatment shots.
- **WhatsApp Usage**: WhatsApp messaging and templates are for South Africa only; US outreach uses SMS/iMessage through the US HighLevel account.
