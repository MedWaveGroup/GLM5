# Follow-up cadence: leads who went quiet after a reply

Machine-readable: `/workspace/objections/followups_1113.json`. Format: each numbered line is its own text.

Applies to: leads who replied once then went quiet (not booked, not opted out, not CPD-price, not Barefoot, not held)

## Rules

- 3 touches max for this cadence; counts toward the 3 automated touches/day cap (sequence messages included)
- Touches only 06:00-22:00 SAST; no touch within 24h of a blast/reactivation; outside WhatsApp 24h window use an approved template
- Rotate variants; never resend a variant to the same lead
- No pricing in follow-ups; CPD-safe by design
- No links; no cure claims
- Stop at once on reply, booking, opt-out, or any hard stop; after touch 3 hand to the owner (Davide)
- Davide's personal touch: after a booked call, the owner can send a short selfie video (Stupar reel); not automated

## Touch 1 — ~24h after the lead's last reply went quiet (next send window)

Goal: Re-open with fresh value, not a chase

**Variant 1**  
Principles: `S-value` Jordan Stupar reel: offer something of value to send ('a video that shows what we do') to earn the next step; `M-solution` Miner NEPQ: solution-awareness question, let them picture the outcome; `M-commit` Miner NEPQ: commitment question as a choice of two times
1. Hi {first_name}, picking up where we left off.
2. One thing I didn't mention, we train your whole team, not just you.
3. So it runs smoothly even on your busiest days.
4. Want me to show you how that looks, {slot1} or {slot2}?

**Variant 2**  
Principles: `S-human` Jordan Stupar reel: humanise, put a face to the name (selfie video after the call), stand out from other vendors; `H-analogy` Hormozi reel: answer objections with an analogy, not an explanation; `M-commit` Miner NEPQ: commitment question as a choice of two times
1. Hi {first_name}, Davide here from MedWave 🙂
2. Putting a face to the name, I'm a real person on this side.
3. Happy to answer anything you were still wondering about.
4. Shall we chat {slot1}, or does {slot2} fit better?

## Touch 2 — ~3 days after touch 1, if still no reply

Goal: New angle: their problem and the cost of waiting

**Variant 1**  
Principles: `M-consequence` Miner NEPQ: soft consequence question (what happens if nothing changes); `H-water` Hormozi reel: the water won't get warmer; waiting doesn't improve the decision; `M-commit` Miner NEPQ: commitment question as a choice of two times
1. {first_name}, quick thought.
2. Every month we wait is a month of patients you could already be helping.
3. Is that something you'd like to change this year?
4. If yes, I've kept {slot1} and {slot2} open.

**Variant 2**  
Principles: `T-clarity` Thiago Reis reel, door 2 clarity: can you see the solution solving your problem?; `H-recipe` Hormozi reel: follow the recipe and you get the cake; the steps work regardless of who you are; `M-commit` Miner NEPQ: commitment question as a choice of two times
1. Hi {first_name}, one for you.
2. Practices that follow our setup steps tend to get going fastest.
3. I can show you the exact steps for a practice your size.
4. Is {slot1} good, or {slot2}?

## Touch 3 — ~5-7 days after touch 2; last automated touch, then hand to owner

Goal: Honest break-up: give the out, keep the door open

**Variant 1**  
Principles: `D-blame` Davide (objection bot): take the blame, 'I haven't given you anything to be interested in'; `T-priority` Thiago Reis reel, door 1 priority: is this a priority now? if not, close the door without pushing; `M-neutral` Miner NEPQ: neutral, detached tone, no pressure, give them the out; `M-commit` Miner NEPQ: commitment question as a choice of two times
1. {first_name}, I've tried calling and texting and haven't heard back.
2. Where do we go from here?
3. If now's not the time, just say so and I'll close the file.
4. If it is, grab {slot1} or {slot2}.

**Variant 2**  
Principles: `T-ready` Thiago Reis reel: don't convince anyone; advance only those ready to buy; `M-neutral` Miner NEPQ: neutral, detached tone, no pressure, give them the out; `M-commit` Miner NEPQ: commitment question as a choice of two times
1. Hi {first_name}, I don't want to keep filling your inbox.
2. Is this still a priority for your practice?
3. A simple yes or no is perfect.
4. And if it's a yes, would {slot1} or {slot2} work?
