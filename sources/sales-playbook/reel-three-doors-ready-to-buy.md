## Gist
- His best seller converts at twice the rate by closing three doors in order.
- 1 Priority: is solving this a priority now? If no, close it without pushing. 2 Clarity: show how it's solved, ask 'can you see your problem solved with this?' 3 Investment: only then price; is it smaller than the cost of keeping the problem?
- He convinces no one; he only advances those already ready to buy.

## How MedWave applies it
- Don't quote before priority and clarity are confirmed (except Davide's approved cash line on request).
- Price framing: investment vs cost of keeping the problem.
- Give the out; spend effort on ready leads.
