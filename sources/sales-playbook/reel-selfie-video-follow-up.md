## Gist
- After the call, ask 'where can I text that to?' while offering a useful video, and you get the cell number.
- Immediately send a personal selfie video: put a face to the name, show you're a real person.
- Claims ~400% more engagement and ~50% first-call close (his claim, unverified).
- Market differentiation: other vendors don't do it.

## How MedWave applies it
- Owner (Davide/closer) can send a 20-second selfie video after a booked or held call. Personal, not automated.
- Offer something of value to send, to earn the WhatsApp number and next step.
