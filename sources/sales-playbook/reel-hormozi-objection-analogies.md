# Reel: Stakes are high: you're too expensive

## Gist
- Overcome objections with analogies, not explanations.
- Too expensive: you don't want the cheapest surgeon/tattoo artist/parachute; high stakes + hard to un-make = best, not cheapest.
- Let me think about it: the water won't get warmer; waiting doesn't improve the decision.
- Will it work for me: follow the recipe, get the cake. How long: can't build muscle in a week. Can I do part: what's the most important part of a car? You need all of it (or sell the bottleneck if you fix one thing).

## How MedWave applies it
- Price → stakes analogy; think → water; does it work → recipe; how long → muscle; partial → whole system.
