# Jeremy Miner: NEPQ (Neuro-Emotional Persuasion Questioning)

Used by the objection bot since 3 Nov (method line: "clarify, isolate, then a soft consequence question") and by the 13 Nov playbook.

## Principles we apply
- **Ask, don't tell.** Questions let the lead persuade themselves. One question per message.
- **Neutral, detached tone.** No pressure, no hype; give them the out ("if it's not for you, that's fine").
- **Clarify the objection** before answering: "Expensive compared to what?"
- **Isolate the real reason:** money vs belief in the tech vs timing ("Is it doubt about the tech or more the budget right now?").
- **Problem-awareness:** let them name the problem in their own words ("Which patients would you want to help first?").
- **Solution-awareness:** let them picture the outcome ("What are you hoping it will do for your practice?").
- **Consequence:** soft cost-of-inaction ("If your week stays this packed, what gives first?").
- **Commitment:** finish with a choice of two times, not "let me know".
