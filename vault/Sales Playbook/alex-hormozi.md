# Alex Hormozi: closing principles

## From the reel Davide sent (13 Nov) — [[reel-hormozi-objection-analogies]]
- **Analogies beat explanations.**
- **Too expensive:** cheapest surgeon / parachute; high stakes and hard to un-make → best, not cheapest.
- **Let me think about it:** the water won't get warmer while you wait; waiting just costs time.
- **Will it work for me?** Same recipe, same cake. Follow the steps.
- **How long?** You can't build muscle in a week.
- **Can I do just part?** What's the most important part of a car? You need all of it (MedWave: we sell a system, not a box).

## From the objection bot (v1.4)
- **CLOSER:** Clarify, Label, Overview past pain, Sell the vacation, Explain away concerns, Reinforce.
- **Onion of blame:** objections blame circumstances (time, money), then others (partner), then self (doubt). Peel in that order.
- **Value before price:** raise the dream outcome and the likelihood it works before any figure.
