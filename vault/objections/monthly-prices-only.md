# Lead-facing prices are monthly amounts only, never full list prices

Effective 12 Nov 2026 10:21 SAST, never quote full equipment prices to leads. When a price may be given (confirmed patients, or anyone else who is not under the CPD hold), use only the monthly amounts below, excluding VAT, with no deposit. Do not mention the interest rate, the term length, or other finance terms in lead-facing wording.

## SUPERSEDED 12 Nov 2026 10:21 SAST — do not send

- Full list / outright prices such as R100,000, R190,000, R100k, R190k, R260,000, R350,000, or "the entry system starts at R100,000".
- The 11 Nov patient price line that quoted R100,000 and R190,000. See [[patient-price]].

## Monthly amounts that may be used (excl. VAT, no deposit)

- Neuro Mobile + stand: about R2,174 a month
- Optimum panel + stand: about R3,805 a month
- Neuro 100-S panel + stand: about R4,131 a month
- Recovery Booth: about R5,436 a month
- Booth + Vibe package: about R8,153 a month

## Rule

- Anyone whose tags contain "cpd" is excluded until they have attended a MedWave online event. See [[cpd-no-pricing]].
- Confirmed patients: use the pending patient price line in [[patient-price]], which quotes the Neuro Mobile and Neuro 100-S monthly amounts and then asks the budget question.
- For everyone else who is allowed a price, quote only these monthly amounts. Never the full equipment price.
- Lead-facing copy may say "about R… a month", "excluding VAT", and "with no deposit". It must not say 11%, 60 months, instalments, payment plan, or other terms.
- Internally these figures are monthly finance amounts. In lead copy do not call out the rate or the term.

## Do not say

- Any full equipment or package list price
- Interest, 11%, 60 months, instalments, "own it for", or other terms
- These monthly amounts to a CPD-list doctor who has not attended a MedWave online event
