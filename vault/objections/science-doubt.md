# Not sure it works / the science

When a lead is not sure the technology works, ask what would make them confident and offer the science summary, then a closer call. Do not use peer-intro or "copy their formula" wording.

## Trigger phrases

- not sure it works
- does the science stack up
- I'm not convinced it will help my patients
- I need to see the evidence

## Rule

- Offer the approved science summary. A request to see it in person goes to a closer call.
- No unsanctioned efficacy claims. No urgency lines. No peer pool intros (HOLD / blocked).

## Approved reply wording

- First reply: "Fair question, and the right one to ask. What would you need to see to feel confident it works for your patients? I can send you the science summary now."
- Follow-up: "Hope the science summary was useful. Would a quick call with Andries or Elmien help? They can walk you through how other practices set up and run sessions day to day."

## Do not say

- "Copy their formula" (blocked)
- Peer introductions (no consented peer pool)
- "Don't wait too long"
- Unapproved medical claims
