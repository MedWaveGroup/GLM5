# Need to think / talk to my partner

When a lead needs to think it over or talk to a partner or shareholders, isolate the open question and offer a joint closer call. Numbers go to Davide or a closer, not into the message.

## Trigger phrases

- I need to think about it
- I have to talk to my partner
- following internal discussions
- a partner is worried about cash flow

## Rule

- Isolate whether the open question is results, space or numbers.
- A joint call is booked with a closer. Do not quote prices or finance in the reply.

## Approved reply wording

- First reply: "Of course, it's a big decision. So I can help you think it through, what's the one thing you're still unsure about: the results, the space, or the numbers?"
- Partner follow-up: "Makes sense. Would it help if your partner joined a 20-minute call with Andries or Elmien, so they hear it first-hand and can ask their own questions?"

## Do not say

- Prices, finance, earnings or legal wording
- Pressure to decide now
