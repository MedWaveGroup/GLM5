# No space

Leads often picture a big fit-out. Ask for a room photo and explain that the panel sits on a stand with castors. Do not quote room sizes that are still on HOLD.

## Trigger phrases

- I don't have the space
- I can't afford it yet and don't have the space either
- the room is too small

## Rule

- Ask for a photo, then offer a closer call.
- Do not quote 0.5 m², 3 m x 4 m, or 2 m x 3 m. Those specs are on HOLD or in conflict.
- The walk-in booth variant is not for use yet (HOLD, pending Davide).

## Approved reply wording

- First reply (C14): "It usually takes less space than people expect. Could you send me a quick photo of the room you have in mind, and we'll check the fit?"
- Follow-up (C4): "The panel sits on a stand with castors, so it rolls away from the bed and back. Would a quick call with Andries or Elmien help, so you can see how it works?"

## Do not say

- Specific plate or room measurements that are on HOLD
- "Fully automated" booth wording
- Prices or finance in the same reply
