# Not now / timing

A timing objection is often hiding doubt about the technology or about money. Isolate which it is, then either nurture or book a closer call. Do not offer financing in the reply.

## Trigger phrases

- very busy at work, will get back to you
- timing is bad, let's talk later
- try next year / put it on hold for now
- moving house / future project
- cash flow tied up, early next year
- can we do this in a few months

## Rule

- Isolate technology doubt versus budget. Budget goes to a closer call. Genuinely next year goes to nurture.
- No finance, flexible financing, or try-before-you-buy wording (blocked / HOLD).

## Approved reply wording

- First reply: "Sure, we can talk in 2029 if that helps 🙂 Just so I understand, is it that you're not sure the technology will help, or is it a budget constraint?"
- Follow-up if budget: "That's sensible. Roughly how many patients do you see a week? Then Andries or Elmien can take you through the options that fit."
- Busy at work, will get back to you: "No rush at all, I know how full clinic days get. When's better for a quick 10-minute call, an early morning or later in the afternoon?"
- On hold / moving / future project: "Thanks for letting me know, and good luck with the move/project. Roughly when would suit you for me to check back?"

## Do not say

- Flexible financing, payment terms, or try-before-you-buy
- "financial constraint" in lead copy; the approved word is "budget"
