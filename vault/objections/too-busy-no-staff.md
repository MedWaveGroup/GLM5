# Too busy / no staff

The lead fears extra workload. Reassure that the package includes delivery, installation and training, then offer a short closer call about running it with minimal staff time.

## Trigger phrases

- too busy
- I don't have the staff
- we haven't got time to run another device
- no one to operate it

## Rule

- Acknowledge the load. Do not offer a fully automated booth angle.
- One question, then a 15-minute closer call if they stay in conversation.

## Approved reply wording

- First reply: "Totally get it. The last thing you need is more on your plate. Every package includes delivery, installation and training for your team. What takes up most of your week right now?"
- Follow-up: "Would a 15-minute call with Andries or Elmien help, so you can see how it's set up with minimal staff time?"

## Do not say

- Full C6 marketing-package wording (on HOLD)
- "Fully automated"
- Prices, finance or earnings
