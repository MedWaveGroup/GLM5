# A cancellation is never a price decline

A lead who cancels a booked slot, or says there is already a MedWave nearby, has not declined the price. Do not treat a cancellation as a patient price objection.

## Trigger phrases

- I need to cancel my appointment
- I can't make the Thursday slot
- please cancel, there's already a MedWave in town
- I have to cancel because there is already a provider in the area

## Rule

- A cancellation is never a price decline.
- Do not send the patient price line just because someone cancelled.
- Do not send the provider-finder link, move them to no-contact, or set DND just because they cancelled or mentioned a nearby provider.
- If they also clearly say a quoted patient price is too expensive, and they are a confirmed patient, then follow [[patient-price]].
- If a practitioner cancels because a provider is already nearby, handle it as [[already-a-provider-nearby]]. If a patient does, notify their owner only; do not send a reply.

## Approved reply wording

- No automatic price or providers-link reply on a cancellation.
- If a practitioner's reason is a nearby provider, use the two approved B2B replies on [[already-a-provider-nearby]]. If a patient raises this, notify their owner only and do not send a reply.

## Do not say

- The patient price line (SUPERSEDED 12 Nov 2026 10:21 SAST: the old R100,000 / R190,000 full-price line, or the new monthly line) in reply to a cancellation
- The providers link as a substitute for handling the real reason
- Anything that closes the file unless they opted out or a confirmed patient declined the price

## Source and date

- 12 Nov 2026, SAST. Chanelle Kemp cancelled a Thursday 08:30 slot because there was already a MedWave in Potchefstroom. A watcher wrongly treated her as a patient who had declined the price, sent the price line and the providers link, then set no-contact and DND. Davide said that should not happen. The nearby-provider rule was added the same morning.
