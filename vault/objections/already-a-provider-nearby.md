# Already a MedWave or provider in my area

"Already a MedWave or another provider in my area" is an objection to handle, not a disqualifier. Never close the lead, set DND, or move them to no-contact for that reason. Many restaurants can share one area. Hundreds of thousands of people need this therapy and there is often only one provider, so MedWave needs to expand. That demand figure stays internal and must not be used in lead copy.

## Trigger phrases

- there's already a MedWave in my town, city or area (for example Potchefstroom)
- I found another provider nearby
- there's already one in my area
- we already have a MedWave here
- someone already offers this near me
- I don't need this, there is already a provider in town

## Rule

- Answer the objection for practitioner and other B2B leads. Do not drop the lead.
- Never close, set DND, or move to no-contact because a provider is already nearby.
- Do not criticise the existing provider. Frame it as capacity and choice, not quality.
- Do not invent statistics. In lead copy say only: "many people in the area need this kind of treatment."
- The two practitioner / B2B replies below were approved by Davide on 12 Nov 2026 at 06:25 SAST.
- The patient reply is not approved. If a patient lead raises this objection, notify their owner only. Do not send them a reply.

## Approved reply wording

Approved 12 Nov 2026, 06:25 SAST, for practitioner and B2B leads only. Under 45 words, one closing question, no sign-off in the body. Add the standard sign-off later if used as a weekday WhatsApp send.

- Practitioner or B2B, restaurant analogy (approved): "Thanks for sharing that. Just as a town supports more than one restaurant, many people in the area need this kind of treatment, and one provider can't serve them all. Would you be open to a short call about bringing it into your practice?"
- Practitioner or B2B, capacity, no analogy (approved): "Thanks for letting me know. A provider nearby is a good start, but many people in the area still need this kind of treatment, and one practice can't serve them all. Would you be open to a short call about bringing it into your practice?"

Patient or consumer: no approved reply. Notify the lead's owner only. Do not send the unused draft, which is kept only in `/workspace/team/outreach-control/objection_provider_nearby_2026-11-12.md`.

## Do not say

- Any price, finance, no-money-down, earnings, ROI, legal or contract wording
- Anything against the other provider
- Medical efficacy claims
- Invented demand statistics, including "hundreds of thousands"
- "our area" as if we are based in the lead's town
- Any reply to a patient lead on this objection; notify the owner only
