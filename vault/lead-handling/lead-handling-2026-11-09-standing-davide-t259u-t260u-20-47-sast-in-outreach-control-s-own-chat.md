Part of [[lead-handling]]. Index: [[index]]. Start: [[Start here]].

- **GIVE, GIVE, GIVE, TAKE is the default for ALL lead outreach.** Davide's words: "I want us to create value 3 times before we ask for an appointment... always use this high value approach to reaching out to leads", then "keeping the wording in the messages minimalistic and valuable". - Give at least 3 separate high-value touches before any call or appointment ask. Examples: the brochure, a practitioner story, the contraindications video, the FAQs page, or a short insight relevant to their practice. - Each touch is short and minimal, gives one new thing, and ends on an open question about them, not about a call. - The 4th touch onward may ask for the call or walkthrough. - Exception: if the lead asks for a call or meeting themselves, book it at once (after the no-double-book check). - This builds on the 1 Nov GGGT standard ("every person should have a high value piece of content given to them three times before we even ask").
