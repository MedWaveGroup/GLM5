# Never send the same or near-identical wording twice

Never send a contact the same or near-identical wording twice. Compare the proposed text against their past outbound messages in HighLevel. This applies to every send, including 1-hour re-touches, which must each add something new.

Cite: Davide Duranti, 12 Nov 2026 10:21–10:22 SAST, relayed by Master Control.
