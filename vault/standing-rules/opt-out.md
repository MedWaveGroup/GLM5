# Opted-out contacts stay, and are never messaged

Keep opted-out contacts in HighLevel with DND on and the Opted out tag. Never delete them and never message them.

Cite: Davide 1 Nov 2026. SA WhatsApp opt-out wording is Reply 1. US SMS is Reply STOP.
