# Skip blasts and reactivation if the lead was contacted in the last 24 hours

Before any blast or reactivation send, skip anyone who received any MedWave message (bot, BDR or closer, any channel) in the last 24 hours. Check HighLevel conversation history, not only our logs. The daily quiet-lead batch counts as reactivation. Reactivation also keeps the stricter 48-hour skip. Do not apply the 24-hour skip to the 1-hour re-touch of brand-new unreplied leads.
