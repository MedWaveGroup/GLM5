# CPD-list doctors get no pricing until they attend an event

Doctors on any CPD list get no pricing of any kind until they have attended a MedWave online event. Identify them by HighLevel tags containing 'cpd' (case-insensitive; it is CPD, not CBD). No price line, no monthly figures, no finance amounts. Only an event invite or a discovery call. Attendance is confirmed by the tag 'cpd - attended'. This is a hold rule, not an opt-out.
