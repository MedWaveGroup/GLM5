# CPD-list doctors get no pricing until they attend an event

Hold pricing for every CPD-tagged contact, with no exemptions. Identify them by HighLevel tags containing 'cpd' (case-insensitive; it is CPD, not CBD) or by the list /workspace/team/outreach-control/cpd_contacts_2026-11-12.csv. No price line, no monthly figures, no finance amounts. Only an event invite or a discovery call. The tag 'cpd - attended' does not count as attending an online event and does not lift the hold, until Davide names a real attendance signal. This is a hold rule, not an opt-out.
