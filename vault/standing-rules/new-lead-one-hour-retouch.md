# One-hour re-touch for brand-new unreplied leads stays on

The 1-hour re-touch cadence for brand-new leads who have not replied stays on. At most 3 automated touches a day, each adding something new, stopping on reply, booking, opt-out or owner pickup. The 24-hour any-communication skip does not apply to this cadence. The never-repeat-wording check still does.
