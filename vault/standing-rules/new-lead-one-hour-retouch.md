# One-hour re-touch for brand-new unreplied leads stays on

The 1-hour re-touch cadence for brand-new leads who have not replied stays on, except that new leads already tagged with an N1 or B1–B5 sequence tag (n1, n1 - b5, b1, b1 - b5, b2, b3, b4, b5) get no one-hour re-touch. For all other new leads: at most 3 automated touches a day, each adding something new, stopping on reply, booking, opt-out or owner pickup. The 24-hour any-communication skip does not apply to this cadence. The never-repeat-wording check still does.

Cite: Davide Duranti, 12 Nov 2026 10:22 SAST, updated 10:42 SAST: no re-touch for sequence-tagged new leads.
