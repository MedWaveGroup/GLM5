# HQ is a closer-calendar booking, not a walkthrough

A booking on a MedWave sales (closer) calendar is HQ unless it is a walkthrough webinar. Closer one-to-ones are for practices seeing over 100 patients a month. Under that, or if volume is unknown, book the group walkthrough first. That is not HQ.
